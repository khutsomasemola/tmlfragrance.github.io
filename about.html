About TML Illuminated

Timeless by design.

A South African home-fragrance brand created around the belief that beautiful spaces deserve beautiful experiences.

Who We Are

We believe your home should feel as beautiful as it looks.

TML Illuminated creates candles and home fragrances designed to transform ordinary spaces into environments filled with atmosphere, warmth and character. We see fragrance as part of the identity of a home — not simply an accessory.

Our Beginning

A passion project became a brand.

TML Illuminated began with a vision to create products that could bring together fragrance, beauty and the feeling of a thoughtfully designed home.

Our first collection represents the beginning of that journey — developed with intention, care and genuine pride in what we are creating.

TML is more than a range of fragrances. It is a vision for a lifestyle where the everyday can feel considered, beautiful and luxurious.

Our Vision

To make everyday spaces extraordinary.

We envision TML becoming part of the spaces people are proud to call home. From a quiet morning to an evening with friends, fragrance has the ability to change how a space feels.

Our goal is to create products that complement the home rather than compete with it — refined, intentional and timeless.
